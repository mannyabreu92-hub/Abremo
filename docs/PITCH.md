# Gabangi: Investor Pitch

**"The Tinder and TikTok of Art."**

A mobile-first platform where users swipe through artwork, discover emerging artists, compete on local and
global rankings, and build reputation through a weighted influence system powered by artists, critics,
collectors and fans.

## The insight
Tinder made finding people addictive. TikTok made finding videos addictive. **Gabangi makes finding art addictive.**
Most art platforms focus on artists. Gabangi focuses on *discovery*.

## Why it could win
- Art discovery is broken
- Social media buries artists
- Galleries are gatekeepers
- Collectors struggle to find new talent
- Younger generations already understand swiping

## The engine
1. **Swipe**: Like, Pass, Super Like (Collect), Follow, Buy.
2. **Weighted Influence**: Fan 1 · Artist 3 · Critic 5 · Collector 9. A collector's like matters more than a fan's, which makes fake popularity expensive.
3. **Charts**: Top Artworks and Top Artists, worldwide and per country, by category, updated hourly. National leaderboards create pride and competition.
4. **Art Battles**: two artworks, one vote, Elo ratings. This is the viral loop.
5. **Badges**: Rising Artist, Top 100 Country, Top 100 Global, Collector Elite, Master Critic, Trend Setter.
6. **AI**: scan any artwork to identify its style, medium and similar artists. The feed learns from every swipe.

## Business model
- Free for artists at launch (upload, swipe, basic rankings)
- Pro $7.99–$19.99/mo: analytics, who liked your work, promotion, portfolio site, sales insights
- 5–10% marketplace commission on sales (the Etsy / Artsy model)

## Key product decision
**The leaderboard is about the artwork first, not the artist.** People fall in love with a painting before they
fall in love with the painter, and an artwork is the unit that gets shared.

## Vision
Become the world's largest art discovery platform and ranking system, where every artist can be discovered and every
collector can find the next Picasso before the world does.
